# Selection — The One-Line Cat Joke That Lands

Gate: one line, under 200 characters, a joke about cats with a setup and a turn.

## Candidates (scored 1–5 against the judge brief, in its order)

1. "My cat knocked my coffee off the table, looked me in the eye, and went back to sleep like a manager after a performance review." — setup (knocked coffee), turn (manager after a performance review), fresh misdirection, retellable. **WINNER**
2. "The cat sat on my keyboard during a work call and somehow typed a better email than I did." — keyboard gag, but the "better than I did" payoff is a known shape; loses to #1 on criterion 3.
3. "My cat has never paid rent, yet she inspects every room I clean like a landlord with a clipboard." — fresh landlord angle, but the joke is an observation, not a turn that lands; loses to #1 on criterion 2.
4. "At 3am my cat sprints down the hallway for no reason. I checked: she has no reason. She just likes the sound." — zoomies are relatable, but "likes the sound" is a shrug, not a laugh; loses to #1 on criterion 2.
5. "I bought my cat a $60 bed. She sleeps in the box it came in, on top of my laptop, during my deadlines." — three beats (box, laptop, deadlines) make it funny, but three beats also pad it; loses to #1 on criterion 4.
6. "My cat stares at the wall for twenty minutes, then walks away. I now pay rent to live in her mystery." — "live in her mystery" is a fresh angle, but it reads clever rather than laugh-out-loud; loses to #1 on criterion 2.
7. "The cat knocked a glass off the shelf and watched it fall like a scientist who already knew the result." — scientist angle is good, but "already knew the result" undercuts the surprise; loses to #1 on criterion 3.
8. "My cat brings me dead mice as gifts. I bring her salmon as rent. We both pretend this is a fair trade." — gift/rent trade is a neat structure, but the payoff is a wry observation, not a laugh; loses to #1 on criterion 2.
9. "I told my cat she was adopted. She blinked slowly, which I have decided means she already knew." — slow blink is cute, but the joke leans on the owner's decision, not the cat; loses to #1 on criterion 2.
10. "My cat's favourite toy is the $200 scratching post. Her second favourite is the $200 sofa next to it." — price contrast is retellable, but it is a one-note observation with no turn; loses to #1 on criterion 1 (setup without a turn).

## Duel summary

#1 beats #3 and #8 on criterion 2 (its final clause is a laugh, not an observation), beats #2 and #7 on criterion 3 (the manager comparison is a misdirection nobody sees coming, not a known shape), and beats #5 on criterion 4 (one turn, no extra beats). No other candidate wins a criterion against #1. Worn puns (cat-astrophe, purr-fect) were excluded before duelling. The "looked me in the eye" beat is what makes the manager comparison land — the cat is not an accident, it is an evaluator.

## Winner

"My cat knocked my coffee off the table, looked me in the eye, and went back to sleep like a manager after a performance review." (emitter-verified character count in test.ts)
